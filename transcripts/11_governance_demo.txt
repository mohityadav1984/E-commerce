=== Application layer: risk classification ===
RISK LEVEL: Medium 
The agent is a customer-support assistant, which the Low/Medium/High scheme places in Medium (customer support tickets). It reads order status and order value, which touches transactional but not medical, hiring or financial-account data, so it is not High. It is above Low because a wrong answer can mislead a customer about refunds or returns, which is why it uses grounded retrieval, a refusal threshold, PII masking, an independent review stage, and has no write access to any order.
